For our Database Management Systems Laboratory mid-term project, we had to build a small group project — a [Gram Panchayat System](https://gram-panchayat.sumitk.me/), a multi-user web app to manage the functioning of a Gram Panchayat, with session-based authentication and facilities to view and edit various entities and pull detailed reports on resources.

The project's main deliverable was really the submission report containing the database's ER diagram, so that's where most of the grading focus was. We were a group, and honestly fed up with writing a full-stack implementation end to end, so we knocked out the whole thing in about 5-6 hours once we got going.

I didn't spend much time beautifying the UI — the teaching assistant grading it wasn't going to reward polish over substance, so the effort went into getting the schema and reports right.
